What this version does

The selection engine uses two independent resources: the original English wording imported locally from the eSDScom 5.5 workbooks, and a draft pilot rule pack containing conditions, affected SDS sections and exact catalogue references.

Safety-critical limitations

Rules are not validated against every provision of CLP Annex IV, REACH Annex II, GB legislation or OSHA HazCom 2024. The catalogue itself is not a legal applicability database. No suggestion is marked mandatory or approved. A rule fails closed if its expected wording or structure code is not found exactly.

Classification inputs are taken as supplied and not calculated. Product-specific first aid, exposure controls, waste disposal, PPE and transport classifications must be independently assessed. Missing phrases and zero matches cannot be treated as evidence of safety.

Versioning and provenance

Original phrase text and identifiers are preserved. Core workbooks lack populated official eSDScom phrase IDs, so internal row keys and structure codes must not be presented as official phrase IDs. Rule and source-catalogue versions are tracked separately.

Data privacy and licensing

All processing stays on your computer. No catalogue or formulation data is uploaded to an external service. Original workbooks and generated databases are excluded from GitHub; review the eSDScom licence before redistributing derived datasets.
